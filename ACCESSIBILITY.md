# Accessibility and inclusive participation

Accessibility is part of VOLPAROSSA's intended design. The project has not had a
complete accessibility audit and does not claim conformance across its experimental
applications or upstream interfaces.

Report barriers in the affected repository's issues. Describe the page or task,
expected behavior, browser/device and assistive technology if relevant, and a
minimal reproduction. Screenshots are optional; text reports are welcome. Do not
share a diagnosis, medical record or other personal information. For help locating
the right repository, use [central Discussions](https://github.com/VOLPAROSSA/volparossa/discussions).

Useful contributions include keyboard and screen-reader checks, clearer error
messages, meaningful headings and link names, text alternatives for diagrams,
contrast fixes, reduced-motion behavior and plain-language explanations. Test
small screens and 200% zoom. Explain what you actually tested; automated checks
alone do not prove accessibility.

Translations are welcome. Start with one bounded page or interface and identify
its source revision and language. Preserve technical limitations, safety warnings,
licenses and attribution. Coordinate terminology in the relevant issue rather
than creating a parallel, unmaintained documentation tree. No translation or
particular assistive technology is currently promised ongoing support.
