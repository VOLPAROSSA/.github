# Community code of conduct

VOLPAROSSA welcomes people of different backgrounds, identities, abilities,
experience levels and viewpoints. These expectations apply to project issues,
pull requests, discussions and other spaces maintained by the project.

## Participate with care

- Treat people with dignity. Critique ideas and evidence, not personal identity.
- Welcome questions and help make explanations understandable.
- Respect consent, accessibility needs, privacy and reasonable personal boundaries.
- Disclose uncertainty, credit contributors and upstream work, and correct mistakes.
- Keep discussion relevant. Respect a request to stop an unwelcome interaction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation and spam are unacceptable. Do not publish someone's private
information, screenshots or communications without permission. Technical or
ethical disagreement, expressed respectfully, is welcome.

## Report a concern

For a private conduct concern, contact **contact@volparossa.org**, the address
published on the organization's GitHub profile. Share the minimum needed to
identify the incident; do not attach secrets or sensitive third-party records.
This is a general project contact, not a confidential whistleblowing service.
For vulnerabilities use [the security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful content, warn a
participant, limit participation or exclude repeated or serious abuse. Actions
should be proportionate to the harm and account for context. A maintainer
involved in a complaint should recuse themselves where another uninvolved
maintainer is available. You may request reconsideration through the same
contact, with relevant context. A small volunteer project cannot promise an
independent review panel or a fixed response time.

Moderation should protect participants without publishing the reporter's identity
or unnecessary incident details. These rules do not confer authority outside
project-managed spaces or replace applicable law.
